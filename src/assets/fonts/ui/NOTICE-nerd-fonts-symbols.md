embedded terminal.

The WOFF2 was generated with `subset-font` 2.5.0/HarfBuzz by passing every
codepoint in the upstream font's cmap. The format changed from TTF to WOFF2;
the resulting font retains all 10,410 encoded characters and the upstream
family/version metadata.

The exact license shipped in the upstream SymbolsOnly directory is included
as `LICENSE-nerd-fonts-symbols.txt`. Nerd Fonts aggregates glyphs originating
under multiple licenses; the tag-pinned upstream `license-audit.md` is also
included here in full so their attribution and applicable component licenses
travel with the binary.
