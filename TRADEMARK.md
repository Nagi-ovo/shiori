## What you may do

- Build, run and modify Shiori for yourself.
- Mirror or redistribute **unmodified official builds** from the official
  releases, with their name and icons intact.
- Refer to Shiori by name to describe it truthfully, for example in reviews,
  articles, tutorials or a compatibility note.
- Say your fork is "based on Shiori" or "a fork of Shiori".

## What you may not do

- Distribute a modified build under the Shiori or 栞 name or with Shiori's
  icons or logo. Before you publish a fork's builds, in app stores or anywhere
  else, give it a different name, a different app icon and a different bundle
  identifier.
- Use the name or logo in a way that suggests your project, product or
  service is official, endorsed by or affiliated with Shiori.
- Use the name or logo in your own product name, domain name or app store
  listing title.

If you are unsure whether a use is fine, open an issue and ask.
